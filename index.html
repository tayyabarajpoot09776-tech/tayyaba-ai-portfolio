03 / Medicine information

Understand your prescribed medicine.

Search by generic or common brand name to learn what a medicine is used for, its potential benefits, common side effects and important precautions. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.

Examples: Panadol, Tylenol, ibuprofen, Brufen, amoxicillin, metformin, omeprazole, cetirizine, atorvastatin. Generic names may differ by country.

Medicine safety: Never start, stop, share, or change the dose of prescribed medication based on this website. Side effects vary by person. For trouble breathing, facial or throat swelling, fainting, or other severe reactions, seek emergency help immediately. Ask your pharmacist about interactions, allergies, pregnancy, and other medicines.

For detailed, regularly maintained drug information, use MedlinePlus Drug Information (US National Library of Medicine). This demo contains a small educational reference library, not a complete medication database.

06 / Vision

Healthcare knowledge should be easier to navigate.

VITALIS is a healthcare information experience focused on clarity, accessibility, and timely medical guidance. It is a demonstration website, not a certified medical device or clinical service. The goal is to help people ask better questions and know when to seek professional care.

Medical safety: Content is general education, not personalized medical advice. No symptom tool can rule out a serious condition. A licensed healthcare professional must evaluate symptoms, make diagnoses, and recommend treatment.

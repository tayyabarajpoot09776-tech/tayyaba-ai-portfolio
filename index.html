02 / Main Focus

Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

Healthcare AI is the central theme of my portfolio. I am exploring how computational models can support healthcare professionals and researchers while maintaining attention to accuracy, fairness, safety and patient privacy.

Medical Diagnosis Support

Investigating how AI models can help analyze medical information and support clinicians in recognizing patterns that may require further evaluation.

Disease Risk Prediction

Studying predictive machine learning approaches that use appropriate health datasets to estimate risk factors and identify possible trends.

Medical Image Analysis

Exploring the role of computer vision and deep learning in analyzing X-rays, scans and other medical images under professional supervision.

Understanding how structured medical data can be cleaned, analyzed and visualized to uncover insights for research and healthcare planning.
